# Inha University Library (인하대학교 정석학술정보관)

Inha University's main library is officially named the **Jungseok Memorial Library / Jungseok Academic Information Center** (정석학술정보관, Jeongseok Haksul Jeongbogwan), located on the university's main campus in Yonghyeon-dong, Michuhol-gu, Incheon, South Korea. Opened in its current building on September 17, 2003 (replacing an earlier "Central Library," 중앙도서관), it was reportedly the largest university library in Asia at the time of opening, with roughly 3,000 seats and over 1.7 million volumes. It is named after "Jungseok" (정석, "quiet stone"), the pen name of Cho Choong-hoon, founder of Hanjin Group, whose family foundation (now the Jungseok Inha Educational Foundation) governs Inha University. Most detailed information available online is in Korean; the library's own website (lib.inha.ac.kr) is a JavaScript-driven site whose content could not be fully extracted by automated fetching, so this research relies heavily on Korean secondary sources (Namu Wiki, Korean Wikipedia, news/blog sites) supplemented by what could be confirmed from official pages.

## General Information / Overview

- Full name: 정석학술정보관 (Jungseok Academic Information Center / Jungseok Memorial Library), the central library of Inha University.
- Address: 100 Inha-ro, Yonghyeon-dong, Michuhol-gu (formerly Nam-gu), Incheon, South Korea (older records list "253 Yonghyeon-dong, Nam-gu, Incheon 402-751"). [Namu Wiki](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%9D%B8%ED%95%98%EB%8C%80%ED%95%99%EA%B5%90/%EC%BA%A0%ED%8D%BC%EC%8A%A4%20%EB%B0%8F%20%EC%8B%9C%EC%84%A4), [librarytechnology.org](https://librarytechnology.org/library/36748), [culture.blogsailing.com](https://culture.blogsailing.com/1044)
- Inha University itself was founded in 1954; its library traces back to 1955. Database/computerized management of the library began in 1984, with a computerized system implemented by 1987. [librarytechnology.org](https://librarytechnology.org/library/36748)
- The current library building opened September 17, 2003, under the name Jungseok Memorial Library (described in one source as opening "with Millennium LAS," referring to its library automation system at the time). It replaced the university's previous Central Library. [Korean Wikipedia](https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/%EC%A0%95%EC%84%9D%ED%95%99%EC%88%A0%EC%A0%95%EB%B3%B4%EA%B4%80), [librarytechnology.org](https://librarytechnology.org/library/36748)
- At opening it was cited as "Asia's largest university library" of its time. [WebSearch summary of Korean sources, corroborated by Namu Wiki](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%9D%B8%ED%95%98%EB%8C%80%ED%95%99%EA%B5%90/%EC%BA%A0%ED%8D%BC%EC%8A%A4%20%EB%B0%8F%20%EC%8B%9C%EC%84%A4)
- The name "Jungseok" (정석) is the pen name of Cho Choong-hoon, founder of Hanjin Group (parent of Korean Air). The educational foundation overseeing Inha University — the Jungseok and Inha foundations merged in 2013 into the "Jungseok Inha Educational Foundation" (학교법인 정석인하학원) — is named after him, which explains the library's name. This foundation also oversees Inha Technical College, Korea Aerospace University, and other schools. [Namu Wiki: 정석인하학원](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%A0%95%EC%84%9D%EC%9D%B8%ED%95%98%ED%95%99%EC%9B%90), [Korean Wikipedia: 정석인하학원](https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/%EC%A0%95%EC%84%9D%EC%9D%B8%ED%95%98%ED%95%99%EC%9B%90)
- Reported total floor area: approximately 3,633 m². [culture.blogsailing.com](https://culture.blogsailing.com/1044)
- The library serves undergraduate and graduate students and staff; outside visitors (Incheon residents) can obtain temporary/external access permits, generally for materials use rather than reading-room seating. [WebSearch summary of Korean sources](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%9D%B8%ED%95%98%EB%8C%80%ED%95%99%EA%B5%90/%EC%BA%A0%ED%8D%BC%EC%8A%A4%20%EB%B0%8F%20%EC%8B%9C%EC%84%A4), [culture.blogsailing.com](https://culture.blogsailing.com/1044)
- Note: source URLs for the library are inconsistent — lib.inha.ac.kr is the current official domain; an older official campus subpage also exists at inha.ac.kr. [inha.ac.kr subview](http://www.inha.ac.kr/kr/1104/subview.do)

## Key Features

- Was Asia's largest university library at its 2003 opening, a scale claim repeated across Korean secondary sources. [Namu Wiki](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%9D%B8%ED%95%98%EB%8C%80%ED%95%99%EA%B5%90/%EC%BA%A0%ED%8D%BC%EC%8A%A4%20%EB%B0%8F%20%EC%8B%9C%EC%84%A4)
- Uses a modern library automation stack: Ex Libris **Alma** (implemented 2018) as the automation platform, **Primo** (2018) as the discovery interface, and **Ex Libris CDI** (Central Discovery Index, 2018) — all delivered as SaaS by the vendor. This replaced an earlier system ("Millennium LAS," used at the 2003 opening). [librarytechnology.org](https://librarytechnology.org/library/36748)
- Organized into subject-specific "information centers" by floor (Natural Sciences, Humanities, Social Sciences), each with its own gated entry — a specialization structure rather than one undifferentiated open stack. [Namu Wiki](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%9D%B8%ED%95%98%EB%8C%80%ED%95%99%EA%B5%90/%EC%BA%A0%ED%8D%BC%EC%8A%A4%20%EB%B0%8F%20%EC%8B%9C%EC%84%A4)
- Reservation-based system for reading-room seats and group study rooms, managed via a dedicated online booking site (booking.inha.ac.kr) and mobile app ("인하대학교 정석학술정보관" on Google Play and the App Store). [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=kr.ac.lib.inha&hl=en_US), [Apple App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6478385631)
- Has a dedicated Law Library (in the Law School building, 4th floor) and Medical Library (in Jeongseok Building B, 1st floor) as satellite branches under the same library administration. [inha.ac.kr subview](http://www.inha.ac.kr/kr/1104/subview.do)
- An "I-META SPACE" on the 1st floor hosts MR/VR ("metaverse") experience programs run with the university's remote-education support center. [Inha University notice](https://www.inha.ac.kr/bbs/kr/8/35069/artclView.do), [Inha MOOC page](https://mooc.inha.ac.kr/ko/e_learning/workshop/view/10310?p=1&status=all)

## Technology

This section documents the specific technologies underlying Inha Library's services, based on (a) the 2018 Ex Libris implementation record already noted above, (b) direct inspection of the raw HTML and JavaScript served by lib.inha.ac.kr and booking.inha.ac.kr (fetched directly via HTTP rather than through a JS-rendering browser, which is how the SPA's underlying code became readable for this pass), and (c) the identified vendor's own corporate site. Where a claim rests on interpreting obfuscated client-side JavaScript (variable/function names, API routes, config flags) rather than an explicit vendor statement, this is flagged as inferred-from-code rather than confirmed-by-documentation.

**1. Book/collection management & cataloging (ILS)**
- The library's automation platform was implemented in 2018 as **Ex Libris Alma** (ILS), **Primo** (discovery), and **Ex Libris CDI** (Central Discovery Index), replacing an older "Millennium LAS" system used since the 2003 building opening. [librarytechnology.org](https://librarytechnology.org/library/36748)
- However, direct inspection of the live site's compiled Angular JavaScript bundle (`https://lib.inha.ac.kr/main.1b4b9f2e314a3490.js`, fetched 2026-09-21) found **no occurrences of "Alma" or "CDI"**, and only one "Primo" match — a false positive from an unrelated device-detection regex listing MSI "Primo" tablet model numbers. This means the current public-facing site is **not** running an Ex Libris Primo VE front end today; instead it is a custom Angular single-page app calling a large set of proprietary REST endpoints (`/api/books/`, `/api/items/`, `/api/holds`, `/api/recalls`, `/api/patron-collections`, `/api/reservation-requests`, `/api/ibl-requests`, `/api/dds-requests`, etc.). Whether Alma still operates as a backend ILS behind this custom layer (a common integration pattern via Alma's open APIs) or has since been replaced could not be confirmed either way — flagged as an open question rather than assumed. [Direct inspection of lib.inha.ac.kr HTML/JS, 2026-09-21](https://lib.inha.ac.kr/)
- The custom front end and its API were built by **INEK Corp.** (아이넥), a Korean "e-library" (전자도서관) solutions vendor that self-describes as leading that market with ~20 years of experience. INEK's product line includes: **SOLARS** (integrated library management system — "도서관의 모든 업무를 통합 관리할 수 있는 시스템"), **PYXIS** (responsive-web patron/discovery interface for PC/tablet), **UMOBILE** (mobile access to library services), and **SMUF v4.0** (GS Grade-1-certified facility/seat reservation management module). INEK's own project-history page lists Inha University engagements in **2020** ("웹표준기반 통합전자도서관시스템 구축(좌석배정포함)" — web-standards-based integrated e-library system build, including seat assignment), **2021** (smart-card management system upgrade including "좌석배정키오스크 7대," seven seat-assignment kiosks), and **2024** ("인하대학교 통합도서정보시스템 고도화" — Integrated Library Information System enhancement). This matches an `/api/smuf-dashboard` endpoint found in the live JS bundle, and a Korean web-search summary that directly states the seat-reservation system was developed by INEK Corp. [inek.kr](https://www.inek.kr/), [inek.kr/about/performance](https://www.inek.kr/about/performance/), [WebSearch summary](https://inha-library.andro.io/), [Direct inspection of lib.inha.ac.kr JS bundle, 2026-09-21](https://lib.inha.ac.kr/)
- **Classification system (KDC vs. DDC) and book barcode-vs-RFID tagging:** not found / no public source. No Korean- or English-language source, nor any string in the inspected JS bundle, confirmed which classification scheme is used or whether individual books carry barcodes only or RFID/NFC tags (see borrowing/returns below for a related but distinct finding about NFC use in the mobile checkout flow). General context, not confirmed for Inha: Korean university libraries built or renovated in the 2000s–2020s commonly use KDC (Korean Decimal Classification) for Korean-language materials and DDC for some Western-language holdings, and increasingly use RFID book tags — but this is a general pattern, not a confirmed Inha fact.

**2. Discovery/search interface**
- As above, the public catalog search today is served by a custom-branded Angular application (not an Ex Libris Primo VE skin), with INEK's **PYXIS** identified as the likely commercial product behind this patron-facing layer. [inek.kr](https://www.inek.kr/), [Direct inspection of lib.inha.ac.kr, 2026-09-21](https://lib.inha.ac.kr/)
- The app includes a **UniDocs viewer** ("Unidocs" appears repeatedly in the JS bundle as `openUnidocsViewer`, `checkIfShowUnidocsViewer`, and a config flag `SEARCH.USE_UNIDOCS_VIEWER`), indicating use of the Korean UniDocs secure/DRM document-viewer product for viewing PDFs or delivered documents (e.g., interlibrary-loan or document-delivery-service files) in-browser rather than downloading raw files. [Direct inspection of lib.inha.ac.kr JS bundle, 2026-09-21](https://lib.inha.ac.kr/)
- No evidence of AI-powered or semantic search, federated cross-database search branding, or "big data" recommendation features was found in the inspected JS bundle (explicit searches for terms like semantic, 인공지능/AI, 챗봇/chatbot, and 빅데이터 all returned zero matches). **Not found / no public source** for any AI/semantic search claim; treat as absent rather than merely undocumented.

**3. Borrowing & returns technology**
- The mobile app supports **self-service checkout initiated from the phone**: client-side code shows a "charge" flow (Korean UI confirms "정상적으로 대출되었습니다" — "successfully checked out") triggered either by scanning a book's barcode with the phone camera (`chargeModule: "barcode"`) or by tapping the phone to the book (`chargeModule: "NFC"`, via functions named `chargeByNfc`, `getBookInfoByCode`, `nfcBookCode`), after which the book's title/author/publisher/barcode are displayed for confirmation. Related function names (`isNFCWriteDone`, `callByAppOfAfterWriteSecurityTag`, and a user-facing prompt to "재태깅해주세요" — "please re-tap" — for security release) suggest the phone's NFC radio also writes back to a tag on the book, consistent with deactivating/rewriting a combined RFID/NFC security tag as part of checkout. This is inferred from reading the app's obfuscated JavaScript directly, not from an explicit vendor description of the feature, so treat the "security tag" interpretation as a reasoned inference rather than a confirmed spec. [Direct inspection of lib.inha.ac.kr JS bundle, 2026-09-21](https://lib.inha.ac.kr/)
- A physical **unmanned seat-ticket kiosk (무인좌석발급기)** exists: per a web-search summary of the library's own student-guide page, a student taps their (RFID) student ID at the reading-room gate, then obtains a seat ticket from an unmanned kiosk, valid for 4 hours (extendable once). INEK's project history separately confirms a 2021 deployment of "7 seat-assignment kiosks" (좌석배정키오스크 7대) for Inha. [WebSearch summary of lib.inha.ac.kr/guide/user/student](https://lib.inha.ac.kr/guide/user/student), [inek.kr/about/performance](https://www.inek.kr/about/performance/)
- **Unmanned/automated book-return machines (무인반납기), book sorting systems, and any robotic/automated storage-and-retrieval system (ASRS):** not found / no public source, despite targeted Korean-language searches (e.g., "무인반납기," "셀프대출기," "도서 소독기"). General context, not confirmed for Inha: unmanned return machines and UV book-sanitizing units are common in large Korean university libraries of comparable scale, but no source or on-site evidence confirms their presence at Inha specifically.

**4. Room/seat reservation technology**
- The seat/room booking platform behind booking.inha.ac.kr and the reservation mobile app is a commercial product, not an in-house build: it is **INEK Corp.'s SMUF v4.0** facility-reservation module (GS Grade-1 certified), evidenced by both INEK's own project listing and an `/api/smuf-dashboard` endpoint present in the live site's JavaScript. [inek.kr](https://www.inek.kr/), [Direct inspection of lib.inha.ac.kr JS bundle, 2026-09-21](https://lib.inha.ac.kr/)
- booking.inha.ac.kr and lib.inha.ac.kr serve the **identical Angular application shell** (same title, scripts, and inline JS, confirmed by direct HTTP fetch of both homepages), indicating the booking site is not a separately built product but a routed section of the same INEK-built portal. [Direct inspection, 2026-09-21](https://booking.inha.ac.kr/)
- The dedicated Android reservation app (package `kr.ac.inha.library`, distinct from the main library app's `kr.ac.lib.inha` package) is published by **INEK Corp.**, is 13 MB, and — per its store description — lets users "reserve general reading rooms using **beacon technology**," book group study rooms and multimedia centers, and carry a **mobile reading pass** for facility entry; it explicitly requires location permission "to use the seat assignment feature," implying **Bluetooth-beacon-based indoor positioning** is used to assign/verify a specific physical seat. [WebSearch summary of inha-library.andro.io](https://inha-library.andro.io/), [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=kr.ac.lib.inha&hl=en_US)
- A December 2024 iOS app update (v1.0.2) added "NFC functionality for space reservation confirmation" (공간예약 배정확정을 위한 NFC 기능 추가), i.e., an NFC tap-based confirmation step layered onto the existing beacon-based seat assignment. [Apple App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6478385631)
- The mobile "membership card" (library card) used within the app is **QR-code-based** (`MEMBERSHIP_CARD_TYPE: "qrcode"` in the site's config), rather than barcode or NFC, for on-screen presentation at desks/gates. [Direct inspection of lib.inha.ac.kr JS bundle, 2026-09-21](https://lib.inha.ac.kr/)
- Seat-occupancy sensors and environmental (IoT) sensors specific to the reading rooms: not found / no public source — the "beacon" evidence above supports indoor positioning/seat-assignment, not occupancy sensing, and should not be conflated with it.

**5. Access control & identification**
- Entry to the reading rooms uses **RFID student ID card recognition** at a gate, after which the unmanned kiosk described above issues a timed seat ticket. [WebSearch summary of lib.inha.ac.kr/guide/user/student](https://lib.inha.ac.kr/guide/user/student)
- Inha's standard student ID is issued jointly with **KEB Hana Bank** as a combined smart card: campus ID + building-access card + T-money-style transportation card + (optionally) a bank debit/checking card, i.e., a "financial student ID" vs. an access/ID-only "non-financial student ID" (비금융학생증) option for students who decline the banking function. [dept.inha.ac.kr building-access guide](https://dept.inha.ac.kr/user/boardList.do?command=view&page=1&boardId=5311146&boardSeq=5510299), [WebSearch summary](https://internationalcenter.inha.ac.kr/bbs/internationalcenter/2507/171118/artclView.do)
- Since a physical card is optional, Inha also offers a **mobile student ID** for building/library access: on Android this uses the phone's NFC-and-payment (HCE-style) mode, and on iPhone it uses Bluetooth (since iOS restricts third-party NFC reader/HCE access) — both accessed via a "Mobile Access Card" button inside a student-ID app. This is a university-wide identification system rather than a library-specific one, but it is the credential the library gates rely on. [WebSearch summary of internationalcenter.inha.ac.kr](https://internationalcenter.inha.ac.kr/bbs/internationalcenter/2507/171118/artclView.do)
- Turnstile hardware make/model: not found / no public source.

**7. Website/app technology**
- lib.inha.ac.kr and booking.inha.ac.kr are both built as an **Angular single-page application** (confirmed directly: the HTML loads `runtime.*.js`, `polyfills.*.js`, `main.*.js` as ES modules, mounts a custom `<ik-root>` element, and the compiled JS contains Angular's Ivy runtime markers such as `ɵfac`/`ɵprov` and `NgZone`-based `zone.run` calls). This is the reason earlier research passes using a JS-unaware fetcher could not retrieve page content; a direct `curl`-style HTTP fetch of the HTML and JS assets (used for this pass) can retrieve them, but a further rendering step would be needed to see the fully rendered DOM. The `<ik-root>` custom element name and an `ikc-accessibility` CSS class visible in the compiled code both point to **INEK Corp.** ("IK") as the builder, consistent with the vendor identification above. [Direct inspection of lib.inha.ac.kr HTML/JS, 2026-09-21](https://lib.inha.ac.kr/)
- Third-party services loaded directly by the site include **Google Analytics (gtag.js)**, the **Kakao Maps SDK** and **Kakao postcode/address-search widget** (`dapi.kakao.com`, `t1.kakaocdn.net`), and the **Iamport** payment SDK (`cdn.iamport.kr/v1/iamport.js`) — a common Korean payment-gateway aggregator, suggesting the site can process card/PG payments somewhere in its flows (e.g., fines, membership fees), though which specific flow uses it was not confirmed. [Direct inspection of lib.inha.ac.kr HTML, 2026-09-21](https://lib.inha.ac.kr/)
- The site also implements a **custom URL scheme** `inhalibapp://sso` for single-sign-on hand-off between the native mobile app and the web app (the web page's `handleOpenURL` function reads SSO parameters passed in via this scheme and stores them in `localStorage`), and defines native-bridge JavaScript functions (`showNotiPopup`, `nfcWriteDone`, `callByAppOfAfterWriteSecurityTag`, `errorOnApp`) that a wrapping native shell calls into the web content — indicating the mobile apps are **native wrappers embedding this same web app for at least some screens** (a hybrid app architecture), rather than being purely native from top to bottom, even though the underlying reservation/catalog logic can also be reached as a plain website. [Direct inspection of lib.inha.ac.kr HTML, 2026-09-21](https://lib.inha.ac.kr/)
- The Google Play listing's package name is `kr.ac.lib.inha` (main library app) while the separate facility-reservation Android app uses `kr.ac.inha.library` — two distinct Android packages/apps from the same vendor (INEK) rather than one app, which is a small but concrete distinction not previously captured. [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=kr.ac.lib.inha&hl=en_US), [WebSearch summary of inha-library.andro.io](https://inha-library.andro.io/)

- No stroller rental, no pet access, and no credit-card payment are available on-site, per a public facilities-info listing — a small but concrete practical detail. [culture.blogsailing.com](https://culture.blogsailing.com/1044)

## Building / Architecture Features

- Reported total floor area of approximately 3,633 m². Number of above-ground/below-ground floors and exact construction budget could not be confirmed from available sources. [culture.blogsailing.com](https://culture.blogsailing.com/1044)
- The building has at least a basement level (housing reading rooms and amenities) plus floors 1 through 4 or 5 above ground (floor-by-floor layout below). [Namu Wiki](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%9D%B8%ED%95%98%EB%8C%80%ED%95%99%EA%B5%90/%EC%BA%A0%ED%8D%BC%EC%8A%A4%20%EB%B0%8F%20%EC%8B%9C%EC%84%A4)
- Floor layout as described by Korean sources:
  - Basement (B1): Reading rooms (over 1,300–1,000+ seats across multiple rooms) and rest/amenity spaces (convenience store, copy shop, vending machines, ATM).
  - 1st floor: "Jungseok Lounge" (정석라운지) with computer terminals, plus a reading area and the "I-META SPACE."
  - 2nd floor: Natural Sciences subject information center (strong in computer-science-related holdings).
  - 3rd floor: Humanities subject information center.
  - 4th floor: Social Sciences subject information center.
  - Floors 1–4: 36 group study rooms distributed across these floors (reservation-based).
  - 5th floor (per one source): "Research Space" and additional group study rooms.
  [Namu Wiki](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%9D%B8%ED%95%98%EB%8C%80%ED%95%99%EA%B5%90/%EC%BA%A0%ED%8D%BC%EC%8A%A4%20%EB%B0%8F%20%EC%8B%9C%EC%84%A4), [WebSearch summary of Korean queries on study rooms](https://lib.inha.ac.kr/guide/guide/rules)
- Architectural/design commentary is limited to secondhand description: the ground-floor lobby is often described as resembling a courthouse/prosecutor's office, which is why it's used as a filming set; no official architect name, design philosophy, or award information could be found in available sources. [Namu Wiki](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%9D%B8%ED%95%98%EB%8C%80%ED%95%99%EA%B5%90/%EC%BA%A0%ED%8D%BC%EC%8A%A4%20%EB%B0%8F%20%EC%8B%9C%EC%84%A4)
- **Gap:** No source found gives the architect/design firm, exact floor count, construction cost, or any award/heritage recognition for the building.

  - No more recent (2020s) total-volume figure could be found in available sources.
  - **Note:** these two figures come from different secondary sources and could not be cross-verified against a single authoritative current count; the library's live catalog statistics were not accessible via WebFetch.
- Subject organization by floor: Natural Sciences (2F, including computer science-related titles), Humanities (3F), and Social Sciences (4F), each in a separate subject information center with its own gate; books must be returned to their originating floor/section. [Namu Wiki](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%9D%B8%ED%95%98%EB%8C%80%ED%95%99%EA%B5%90/%EC%BA%A0%ED%8D%BC%EC%8A%A4%20%EB%B0%8F%20%EC%8B%9C%EC%84%A4)
- Special/branch collections: a dedicated **Law Library** (법학전문도서관, in the Law School building) and a **Medical Library** (의학도서관, in Jeongseok Building B) function as specialized branches with law- and medicine-specific holdings. [inha.ac.kr subview](http://www.inha.ac.kr/kr/1104/subview.do)
- The library offers electronic journals, academic databases, e-books, and multimedia materials (video/audio/DVD), plus "preservation storage" for older/rare materials, per the official campus overview page. [inha.ac.kr subview](http://www.inha.ac.kr/kr/1104/subview.do)
- Separate university-run e-book platforms exist under the Inha domain (e.g., yes24.inha.ac.kr, ebook.inha.ac.kr, bookcube.inha.ac.kr), suggesting partnerships with commercial e-book vendors (Yes24, Bookcube) for digital lending, though the specific scope of each wasn't confirmed in depth. [yes24.inha.ac.kr](https://yes24.inha.ac.kr/), [ebook.inha.ac.kr](https://ebook.inha.ac.kr/), [bookcube.inha.ac.kr](https://bookcube.inha.ac.kr/)
- Interlibrary loan/exchange programs exist with domestic and international institutions, per the official overview, though details on partner institutions were not found. [inha.ac.kr subview](http://www.inha.ac.kr/kr/1104/subview.do)
- **Gap:** No current, single authoritative total volume count, no breakdown of database subscriptions (e.g., which specific databases like Web of Science, RISS, KISS, etc. are offered) could be confirmed.

## Room Types Available

- **Reading rooms** (열람실): Located in the basement, split across at least three rooms:
  - Room 1: up to 409 seats
  - Rooms 2-1 and 2-2 (divided by glass partition): 513 seats combined
  - Room 3: up to 409 seats
  - Combined basement reading-room seating is cited as "over 1,300 seats" in one source and "1,000+ seats" in another (likely reflecting different counting methods or time periods).
  [Namu Wiki](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%9D%B8%ED%95%98%EB%8C%80%ED%95%99%EA%B5%90/%EC%BA%A0%ED%8D%BC%EC%8A%A4%20%EB%B0%8F%20%EC%8B%9C%EC%84%A4)
- **Group study rooms** (그룹스터디룸): 36 rooms distributed across floors 1–4, reservation-based, popular for team/group project work. [Namu Wiki](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%9D%B8%ED%95%98%EB%8C%80%ED%95%99%EA%B5%90/%EC%BA%A0%ED%8D%BC%EC%8A%A4%20%EB%B0%8F%20%EC%8B%9C%EC%84%A4)
- **Research Space** (5th floor, per one source): a dedicated area distinct from undergraduate group study rooms, along with additional group study rooms on that floor. [WebSearch summary](https://lib.inha.ac.kr/guide/guide/rules)
- **Jungseok Lounge** (정석라운지, 1st floor): an open computer-lab-style lounge area rather than a bookable private room (see Facilities below).
- **Subject information centers** (2F–4F): open-stack reading/browsing areas organized by subject (Natural Sciences, Humanities, Social Sciences), each behind its own gate.
- **Law Library** and **Medical Library**: separate branch reading rooms located outside the main building (Law School building 4F; Jeongseok Building B, 1F).
- **Gap:** No source specifies dedicated named seminar rooms, a distinct "media room," or formal exhibit/gallery rooms as separate room types beyond what's listed above; the official site likely has a fuller room list but its JS-rendered pages could not be scraped.

## Room Features

- Group study rooms and reading-room seats are reservation-based, bookable via an online reservation and on-site ticketing system, with a companion mobile app (Android/iOS) and a dedicated booking web portal (booking.inha.ac.kr). [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=kr.ac.lib.inha&hl=en_US), [Apple App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6478385631)
- The library implements a "renewal/reconfirmation system" for all-day reading-room passes — users must periodically re-confirm their seat/pass to keep it, intended to prevent seat-squatting and keep turnover fair. [culture.blogsailing.com](https://culture.blogsailing.com/1044), [WebSearch summary](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%9D%B8%ED%95%98%EB%8C%80%ED%95%99%EA%B5%90/%EC%BA%A0%ED%8D%BC%EC%8A%A4%20%EB%B0%8F%20%EC%8B%9C%EC%84%A4)
- "Reading room supervisors/monitors" enforce quiet — students who disrupt the reading-room atmosphere reportedly can receive penalty notices/warnings. [Namu Wiki](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%9D%B8%ED%95%98%EB%8C%80%ED%95%99%EA%B5%90/%EC%BA%A0%ED%8D%BC%EC%8A%A4%20%EB%B0%8F%20%EC%8B%9C%EC%84%A4)
- Only water/beverages are allowed inside reading rooms; food is strictly prohibited. [Namu Wiki](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%9D%B8%ED%95%98%EB%8C%80%ED%95%99%EA%B5%90/%EC%BA%A0%ED%8D%BC%EC%8A%A4%20%EB%B0%8F%20%EC%8B%9C%EC%84%A4)
- Room capacities (seat counts) are documented for reading rooms (409 / 513 / 409 as noted above) but individual group-study-room capacities (e.g., 4-person vs. 8-person rooms) could not be confirmed from available sources.
- **Gap:** No details found on in-room equipment (screens/monitors, whiteboards, video-conferencing gear) for group study rooms specifically.

## Opening Times / Hours

Note: hours reported below come from secondary/aggregated web-search summaries rather than a direct reading of the official hours page (lib.inha.ac.kr/guide/guide/rules), since that page is JavaScript-rendered and its content could not be extracted directly. Treat exact times as indicative and verify on the official site before relying on them.

- **General library/reading-room hours (per aggregated search results):**
  - Weekdays (Mon–Fri), during the semester: 09:00–21:00
  - Saturday, during the semester: 09:00–18:00
  - Sunday: closed
  - During vacation periods, hours are reduced: weekdays 09:00–16:00, Saturday 09:00–13:00 (per one aggregated summary).
  [WebSearch summary of lib.inha.ac.kr pages](https://lib.inha.ac.kr/guide/guide/rules)
- **Extended/24-hour reading room access:** A separate extended-hours arrangement allows use up to midnight (24:00) daily, including weekends, for use after the regular reading-room closing times (i.e., after 21:00 on weekdays and after 18:00 on Saturday, and on Sundays). During these extended hours, patrons must use the basement (B1) north-side entrance specifically. [WebSearch summary of lib.inha.ac.kr notices](https://lib.inha.ac.kr/guide/bulletins/notice/950)
- **Vacation-period schedule example:** For a summer vacation period cited as June 23 – August 31 (a specific past year), a separate operating-hours notice was posted; the exact hours were not extracted, only that a distinct vacation schedule exists and is announced via the library's notice board. [WebSearch summary of lib.inha.ac.kr notices](https://booking.inha.ac.kr/guide/bulletins/notice/285)
- Loan/return service note: material checkout and return is available up to 10 minutes before the relevant desk's closing time, with different final cutoffs on weekdays (18:00) vs. Saturday (13:00) mentioned in one summary — this appears to refer to circulation-desk hours rather than reading-room/building hours, and may not be fully current. [WebSearch summary of lib.inha.ac.kr pages](https://lib.inha.ac.kr/guide/guide/rules)
- **Gap:** No confirmed hours for the Law Library or Medical Library branches specifically (these may differ from the main building). No confirmed holiday-schedule details beyond the general Sunday closure. Because the official hours page could not be rendered, all of the above should be treated as best-available secondary reporting, not a verified primary-source quote.
